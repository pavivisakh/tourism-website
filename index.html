FAQ

Pathanamthitta is a district in the southern Indian state of Kerala. It is known for its lush greenery, rivers, hills, and religious significance.

Pathanamthitta is best known as the gateway to the Sabarimala Temple, one of the world's largest annual pilgrimage destinations. It is also famous for eco-tourism, forests, and cultural festivals.

The best time to visit is between October and February, when the weather is pleasant for sightseeing and outdoor activities.
